\section{Creating an account}
After participant verification, the application will be downloaded on the their device.
The participant will be asked to launch the application and will proceed through each stage below.

\textbf{'Please click on 'create account' to be redirected to the account creation functionality.'} \\
Are the different elements on the screen appropriately positioned? \textbf{YES/NO} \\
\textit{If the participant selects the 'NO' option, please inquire further.} \\
Is the font sufficiently visible?\\
Do the colors used have a good contrast?\\
Are the buttons sized well?\\
Functionality use: \\
Rate the difficulty level for creating an account: low, medium, high \\
Were the visual elements arranged logically?\\
(if not, ask which one)\\
Do you have any comments/improvements for account creation? \\
Once the account has been created, the next step will be navigation to the home screen. Initially, the home screen will be empty.\\
