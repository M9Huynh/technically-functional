\section{\textbf{Introduction}}
The purpose of this questionnaire is to obtain some preliminary information about the participants' experience with 
a knee injury. \\
It will be administered in a structured manner, where participants will be presented the questions 
in the order they were designed. This will ensure consistency and an optimal amount of detail that is relayed 
to the investigators.



\section{\textbf{Participant Information}}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item What is your name?
    \item How old are you?
    \item What gender do you identify with? 
    \item What is your occupation?
    \item Have you ever had a knee injury? \textbf{Yes/No/NA}
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item If \textbf{YES}, was it in the past 5 years?
        \item If \textbf{YES}, have you had physiotherapy after your knee surgery?
        \item If \textbf{NO}, have you had another kind of orthopaedic surgery?
        \item If \textbf{NA}, do you personally know anyone who has experienced a knee injury? \textbf{YES/NO}
    \end{enumerate}
    \item How long did your post-operative physiotherapy last?
    \item Were you prescribed knee extensions as an exercise to do at home?
    \item In your opinion, did you encounter any trouble performing knee extensions? \textbf{YES/NO}
\end{enumerate}
