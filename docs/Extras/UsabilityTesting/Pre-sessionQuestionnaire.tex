The following questions were asked to each participant at the start of their session before they used the application.\\
The first two questions are for verification purposes.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item How old are you?
    \item What is your occupation?
    \item Have you ever had a knee injury? \textbf{Yes/No}
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item If so, was it in the past 5 years?
        \item If not, have you had any other kind of orthopaedic injury?
        \item Have you required surgery?
        \item If so, have you had physiotherapy after surgery or injury?
    \end{enumerate}
    \item What do you use your mobile phone for?  
\end{enumerate}
